Frequently asked questions

What is Last Orbit: Space Defense?

Last Orbit is a free mobile tower-defense strategy game by Yigit Bayram. You command a single Command Ship and a field of invisible mines against endless waves of alien fleets, placing mines before each wave and commanding your escorts in real time during it.

Is Last Orbit free?

Yes. The game is free to download and play. It contains optional ads and optional in-app purchases (removing ads and cargo packs of salvage and dark matter). Nothing in the ranking can be bought.

Which platforms is it on?

iPhone and iPad (iOS 15.0 or later) on the App Store. An Android version is in closed beta and will follow on Google Play.

When is the Android version coming?

The Android build is complete and in Google Play's closed-testing phase. It will be published on Google Play once that phase ends. The package name is com.yigitbayram.lastorbit.game.

Can I play offline?

No. Last Orbit requires an internet connection for the world ranking and the store.

How do mines work?

You place mines on the battlefield before each wave. The enemy cannot see them and their radar cannot detect them. There are five kinds: Contact, Meteor (a rock that blocks paths and fire), EMP (strips shields), Shrapnel (area hull damage) and Mass (pulls ships in, then implodes).

How many waves are there?

There is no last wave. Seven civilisations hold twenty waves each (1–140), then the Heirs rule from wave 141 onward with ever larger fleets.

How does the ranking work?

Every commander in the world is placed by the furthest wave they have reached. You pick a callsign; no account or login is needed.

Which languages does it support?

English and Turkish.

What is the age rating?

12+ on the App Store (fantasy sci-fi violence against spaceships, no blood).

How do I restore purchases?

Open the Store in the game and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought them with.

Who made Last Orbit?

Last Orbit is made by Yigit Bayram, an independent developer. Contact: bayramelmaios@gmail.com.

What data does the game collect?

An anonymous player ID and callsign for the ranking, and the advertising identifier used by AdMob for optional ads. See the privacy policy for details.

Descriptions

One line: Lay invisible mines, command your fleet, hold the last orbit. Endless waves.

Short: Last Orbit is a free mobile tower-defense game where the enemy cannot see your mines. Plan each wave before it arrives, then command a small fleet in real time against seven alien civilisations and the Heirs who follow them.

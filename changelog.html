Updates

What changed in each version of Last Orbit.

1.0.2 · 2026-11-11 · In testing

  • New Journey screen in the hangar: the route travelled, civilisations met, weapons used, the current wave and what lies ahead, with a shortcut to the ranking.
  • One short metallic sound for every purchase: ships, mines, cargo and store products.
  • The wave counter at the top now shows the wave just fought while the wreckage is being collected.

1.0.1 · 2026-11-10 · Released on the App Store

  • Store page links and metadata fixes.
  • Retry when the store cannot be reached.

1.0 · 2026-11-09 · First release on the App Store

  • Endless waves across seven civilisations and the Heirs.
  • Five mine types, seven ship classes, four powers.
  • World ranking by the furthest wave reached.
  • Turkish and English.

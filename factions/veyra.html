Veyra · The Glass Choir

The Veyra sing their shields into being. Their ships are vessels of layered glass and light, and the Choir that steers them is the first civilisation to reach the last orbit. They are the tutorial enemy: their fleets teach the mine lesson, the first admiral and the first meteor belt.

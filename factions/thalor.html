Waves 61–80

Thalor · The Deep Current

The Thalor rise from an ocean world; their ships are grown, not built, and their hulls are the thickest you have met so far. Shields barely matter to them. From wave 61 the admirals of beaten civilisations begin to fight alongside the current ruler, so the fleets grow mixed.

Wave guide

Last Orbit is endless, but it is not random: every wave is derived from its number, so the same wave is the same fight for every commander in the world. This is the structure.

Rhythm inside a block

  • Waves 1–3: the tutorial; the arrival zone is exact and wave 1 is the mine lesson.
  • Waves 2–9: a bigger crowd of lighter ships, so the opening fights are busy but forgiving.
  • Every 10th wave: an admiral ship (1×2, the block's boss). Every 20th wave brings two.
  • Waves 15, 25, 35 …: the civilisation's flagship leads the fleet.
  • From wave 61: admirals of beaten civilisations fight for the current ruler: every third wave, then every second, then every wave, then two at a time from 121.
  • From wave 141: the Heirs. Fleet ceiling 26, two admirals and one more every 25 waves.

Hazards

Meteor belt: from wave 8, every ninth wave (8, 17, 26, 35, 44, 53, 62, 71, 80, 89, 98, 107, 116 …). Meteor mines are your only cover.

Black-hole anomaly: from wave 22, every sixth wave (22, 28, 34, 40, 46, 52, 58, 64, 70, 76, 82, 88, 94, 100, 106, 112, 118 …).

Space-time rift: first tears open by itself on wave 54.

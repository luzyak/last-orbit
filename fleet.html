The fleet

You start alone with the Command Ship and earn the rest by surviving. Ships cost salvage recovered from enemy wrecks, can be repaired and upgraded in the hangar, and answer to three orders in battle: guard, charge, focus fire.

Command Ship · Flagship

Pilot: You, the Commander · unlocks on wave 1

Your only ship at the start and the one you must keep alive. Lose it and the run ends.

Hunter · Fighter

Pilot: Captain Ece Yalın · unlocks on wave 6

Fast escort that chases targets down. Your first recruit; best used to charge what the mines have already weakened.

Bulwark · Armour

Pilot: Bora Demir · unlocks on wave 16

Thick hull built to stand in front and draw fire away from the Command Ship.

Halo · Shield projector

Pilot: İdil Aras · unlocks on wave 28

Projects a shield over nearby ships. Pairs with the Bulwark to hold a line under heavy beams.

Lance · Artillery

Pilot: Mira Sarp · unlocks on wave 38

Long-range, high-damage, fragile. The answer to slow heavy hulls like the Korvath.

Patch · Repair

Pilot: Dr. Deniz Eren · unlocks on wave 58

Repairs hulls mid-fight. Keeps a damaged front line alive through long mixed-fleet waves.

Well · Gravity

Pilot: Prof. Ozan Kutlu · unlocks on wave 64

Bends enemy paths with a gravity field. Pulls fleets onto your mines or away from your ships.

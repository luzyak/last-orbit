Last Orbit: Space Defense

A free tower-defense strategy game for iPhone and Android. The enemy cannot see your mines. Neither can their radar. That is your only advantage.

You have one Command Ship, a field of invisible mines and an endless enemy advancing through the dark. Every wave is a puzzle you set up before it arrives: read the formation, predict the path, lay the trap, then command your escorts in real time while it springs.

Why it plays differently

Every wave is planned first, fought second

You see the incoming fleet, its formation and its arrival zone before it jumps in. The mines you place in that quiet minute decide the fight.

Five mines, seven ships, four powers, one answer per enemy

EMP strips Veyra shields; shrapnel wears Thalor hulls; meteors stall the Khar; Lances punish the slow Korvath. Each civilisation needs a different plan.

Real-time command on top of the plan

Guard, charge or focus fire. When the plan fails, your pilots can still make the final sacrifice.

Hazards that become weapons

Survive a meteor belt, then call one down. Meet a black hole, then open one. Watch the rift tear, then tear it yourself.

Seven civilisations, then the Heirs

Veyra, Nul, Nivara, Thalor, Khar, Auralis and Korvath each hold twenty waves with their own ships and admirals. From wave 141 the Heirs steer them all.

A world ranking

Every commander is placed by the furthest wave they reached. No lives to buy; only the plan counts.
